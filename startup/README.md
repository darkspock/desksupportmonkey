# The AI-Managed Company Experiment

DeskSupportMonkey is not just an IT asset management platform. It is a public experiment in running a software company with AI agents.

**The thesis:** Can a 1-person company with AI agents compete with a 50-person SaaS team?

The founder is the Orchestrator — the only human. Every line of code is written by Claude Code. Every strategy document, market analysis, and piece of content is AI-produced under explicit human direction. The entire company is public on GitHub.

## The Product

DeskSupportMonkey is an IT asset lifecycle management platform. It tracks every device from purchase to decommission, with incidents natively linked to assets.

- **Open source:** AGPL license, self-host for free
- **Cloud version:** Available at [dsmcontrol.com](https://dsmcontrol.com) for companies that want managed hosting
- **Target:** EU SMBs (10-300 employees) facing NIS2, DORA, and CRA compliance requirements

---

## The Business Model (Summary)

- **Revenue:** SaaS subscription, priced by company size (not per-agent, not per-asset)
- **Free tier:** Up to 10 employees, forever
- **Open source:** Unlimited, self-hosted (AGPL)
- **Bootstrapped:** No external funding. Growth funded by revenue and AI efficiency.
- **Team model:** AI agents + human advisors. No employees until revenue clearly justifies it.

For the full commercial strategy, see [dsmcontrol.com](https://dsmcontrol.com).
