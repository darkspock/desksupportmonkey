# DSM Control — Brand Identity

**Date:** 2026-02-24
**Brand type:** Commercial SaaS — compliance-focused IT asset management

---

## Name

**DSM Control**

- "DSM" connects to DeskSupportMonkey without carrying the playful tone
- "Control" is exactly what a regulated IT manager wants: control over assets, control for compliance, control for auditors
- NIS2 Article 21 literally speaks of "control of assets" — the name maps to the regulation
- Works in English and Spanish without translation
- Short, professional, memorable

**Domain:** dsmcontrol.com (available)

---

## Tagline

**Primary:** "Control total de tus activos TIC" / "Total control over your IT assets"

**Alternatives:**
- "IT asset lifecycle management for NIS2 compliance"
- "From purchase to decommission — every device, every incident, every audit"
- "The compliance-ready ITAM platform for European SMBs"

## Color Palette

Light theme. No dark mode on the commercial site.

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Navy | #1a2332 | Headings, navbar background, footer |
| **Accent** | Teal | #0d9488 | CTAs, success states, compliance checkmarks |
| **Accent hover** | Darker teal | #0f766e | Button hover states |
| **Background** | White | #ffffff | Main content background |
| **Surface** | Light gray | #f8fafc | Cards, sections with subtle contrast |
| **Border** | Gray 200 | #e2e8f0 | Dividers, card borders |
| **Text primary** | Slate 800 | #1e293b | Body text |
| **Text secondary** | Slate 500 | #64748b | Captions, secondary information |
| **CTA primary** | Blue 600 | #2563eb | Primary action buttons |
| **CTA hover** | Blue 700 | #1d4ed8 | Button hover |
| **Danger** | Red 600 | #dc2626 | Errors, critical alerts |
| **Warning** | Amber 500 | #f59e0b | Warnings, attention needed |

### Color rationale
- **Navy** conveys authority, security, and trust — the same palette as enterprise tools (IBM, Salesforce)
- **Teal** is the color of compliance and "all clear" — green family without being literal green
- **No orange, yellow, or bright colors** as primary — those signal "fun" or "startup", not "regulated"
- All colors meet WCAG AA contrast requirements on white backgrounds

## Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Size range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Headings** | Inter | 700 (Bold) | 24px - 48px |
| **Subheadings** | Inter | 600 (SemiBold) | 18px - 24px |
| **Body** | Inter | 400 (Regular) | 16px |
| **Captions** | Inter | 400 (Regular) | 14px |
| **Buttons** | Inter | 600 (SemiBold) | 14px - 16px |

### Why Inter
- Clean, modern, highly legible
- Designed specifically for screens
- Used by enterprise SaaS (Linear, Vercel, Stripe)
- Excellent language support (Spanish, German, French — all target markets)
- Free and open source (SIL Open Font License)

**No decorative fonts. No monospace. No serif.** Clean and professional only.

## Logo

### Concept
- **Text-based logo:** "DSM Control" in Inter Bold
- **Optional icon:** Abstract shield + grid pattern (security + inventory control)
- The icon should work as a favicon and app icon at 32x32px

### Rules
- No mascot. The monkey does not appear anywhere on DSM Control.
- No illustrations or cartoon elements
- Logo must work on white background (primary) and navy background (inverted)
- Minimum clear space: height of the "D" on all sides

## Imagery

| Type | Use | Style |
|---|---|---|
| **Product screenshots** | Hero, features, lifecycle | Real screenshots of the product with clean browser chrome |
| **Icons** | Feature grid, benefits | Minimal line icons (Lucide or Heroicons style) |
| **Illustrations** | Never | No abstract illustrations, no isometric graphics |
| **Stock photos** | Sparingly | Only if showing real IT environments (server rooms, office IT setup). Never generic "happy business people" |
| **Diagrams** | Lifecycle, architecture | Clean vector diagrams with brand colors |

## Tone of Voice

| Attribute | DSM Control | NOT this |
|---|---|---|
| **Professional** | "Manage your complete IT asset lifecycle" | "Manage your stuff easily!" |
| **Direct** | "NIS2 requires documented asset control. DSM Control provides it." | "We might be able to help with compliance things" |
| **Confident** | "Full audit trail from day one" | "We try our best to cover compliance" |
| **Clear** | "€99/month for companies up to 100 employees" | "Contact us for custom pricing" |
| **Calm** | "Start complying this week" | "ACT NOW before the audit!" |

### Language
- **Primary:** Spanish (Spain market first)
- **Secondary:** English (EU expansion, open source crossover)
- **Future:** German, Italian (Phase 2 markets)

### Words we use
- Control, trazabilidad, compliance, ciclo de vida, auditoria, activos TIC, inventario, gestion

### Words we avoid
- Monkey, fun, easy, simple, AI-managed, open source (as a selling point — it's mentioned but not the headline), hack, disrupt

## Competitive Visual Positioning

| Competitor | Their style | DSM Control differentiator |
|---|---|---|
| Freshservice | Colorful, playful, lots of illustrations | More serious, compliance-focused |
| Lansweeper | Dark, technical, security-oriented | Warmer but still professional, SMB-friendly |
| GLPI | Dated, PHP-era design | Modern, clean, SaaS-native |
| Snipe-IT | Developer-oriented, GitHub-forward | Business-oriented, buyer-focused |
| ServiceNow | Enterprise, complex, intimidating | Approachable for SMBs, transparent pricing |

**Our position:** Modern and professional like Stripe, but warm and approachable enough for a 30-person company's IT manager who is not a developer.
