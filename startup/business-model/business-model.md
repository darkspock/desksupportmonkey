## What We Sell

DeskSupportMonkey is an IT asset lifecycle management platform. It tracks the complete journey of every device in a company: purchase → warehouse → assignment → incidents → decommission.

It is not a generic helpdesk. Tickets exist but are tied exclusively to assets — every incident is linked to a specific device, creating an auditable history per asset.

**Why companies buy it:**
1. They need to know what devices they have, where they are, and who has them
2. They need to comply with NIS2, CRA, or DORA — which require exactly that information, documented and auditable
3. Spreadsheets and informal processes break down above 10 employees

## The Meta-Product: AI-Managed Company

DeskSupportMonkey is two things simultaneously:

1. An IT asset management platform for NIS2 compliance
2. A public experiment in AI-managed company operations

The founder is the Orchestrator — the only human. Every function — engineering, growth, customer success, operations — is handled by AI agents under explicit human direction. The entire company, including this document, is public on GitHub.

This is not a gimmick. It is the business model thesis: if AI can run a SaaS company at 10% of the traditional cost, the unit economics are unbeatable. We are proving it live.

## Revenue Model

### SaaS Subscription — Monthly, per company size

Pricing is based on number of employees in the client company, not on user seats or asset counts.

| Plan | Price | Company Size | Key Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | €0 | Up to 10 employees | Try the product, very small teams |
| **Starter** | €49/month | Up to 25 employees | Small companies, full feature set |
| **Growth** | €99/month | Up to 100 employees | Mid-size companies, NIS2 compliance |
| **Scale** | €199/month | 100+ employees | Larger companies, full compliance, SSO, audit trail |
| **Open Source** | Free (self-hosted) | Unlimited | Community, self-hosted IT departments |

### Why employee-based pricing
- Aligns cost with the actual scope of the problem — more employees = more devices = more compliance burden
- Simple to understand and verify — no counting seats or assets
- NIS2 compliance value scales with company size — larger companies have more regulatory exposure
- No punishment for adding users or assets — unlimited on all plans

## Team Model: Advisors, Not Employees

### Current state
- 1 human: Orchestrator (founder)
- AI agents: engineering, growth, customer success, operations, content

### When human expertise is needed
We bring in **advisors**, not employees:
- Domain advisors (NIS2 compliance, IT security, cybersecurity)
- Technical advisors (architecture review, external audits)
- Commercial advisors (senior sales, channel partnerships)

Advisors are compensated with equity or a small retainer. No management overhead, no full-time salaries.

### Future hiring — only if revenue justifies it
The question is not "when do we hire?" but "does human hiring outperform expanding AI capabilities?" By late 2026, the answer may be:
- Never — AI agents handle all functions
- Selectively — 1–2 humans in roles where AI cannot replace judgment (enterprise relationships, regulatory representation)
- Traditional — if AI limitations require it

We will follow the evidence. There is no plan to hire X people by date Y.

## NIS2 / Compliance as a Growth Driver

NIS2 became enforceable in October 2024. DORA took effect January 2025. The CRA is phasing in through 2027. Hundreds of thousands of EU companies now have a legal obligation to document and control their IT assets.

DeskSupportMonkey answers the three core questions every NIS2 auditor asks:
1. What devices do you have on your network? → Asset inventory
2. Who has access to what? → Assignment and user management
3. What incidents have those devices had? → Asset-linked tickets and audit trail

**This creates a new sales trigger that competitors don't have:** a company doesn't buy because they want better asset management — they buy because their auditor told them they have to. That is a much shorter sales cycle.
