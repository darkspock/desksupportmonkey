# Go-to-Market Strategy

**Model:** Bootstrapped, open source, build in public
**Budget:** Near zero — growth is funded by time and AI, not ad spend
**Primary narrative:** 100% AI-managed company. One human, one mission, one experiment.
**Primary channel:** The story + GitHub + open source community
**Secondary channels:** Podcasts, events, courses, SEO, LinkedIn, IT communities, PLG

---

## The Core Bet

We are not just selling IT asset management software. We are running a public experiment:

> **Can a 1-person company with AI agents compete with a 50-person SaaS team?**

The founder is the Director de Orquesta — the only human. Every line of code is written by Claude Code. Every strategy document, market analysis, financial projection, and piece of content is AI-produced under explicit human direction. The entire company is public on GitHub.

This story is not a marketing gimmick. It is the truth. And it is the most shareable thing about this company. The moment someone hears "this product was built and is managed entirely by AI, with one human as the orchestrator," they share it. That is free distribution that no ad budget can buy.

## First 6 Months: Awareness Campaign

The first 6 months are about one thing: getting known. Not just as "another ITAM tool" — as the AI-managed company running the experiment live.

### Communication Channels — Month 1–6

#### Social Media (LinkedIn — primary)
- Weekly founder posts documenting the AI orchestration model
- Monthly MRR transparency updates (including €0 months — the journey is the content)
- Content series: "What I delegated to AI this week and what I didn't"
- NIS2 educational content in Spanish targeting IT managers
- Posts about product decisions: "How I used Claude to architect this feature"
- Polls, questions, engagement with IT manager community in Spain

#### Podcasts
Target: tech podcasts, AI/future-of-work podcasts, startup podcasts, IT management podcasts

- **As a guest:** pitch the "AI-managed company" angle — unique enough to get booked without having 10K followers
- Target shows (Spain + international): Startup Podcast, AI Uncut, La Startup Española, Lex Fridman-adjacent shows, r/MachineLearning adjacent content
- Message: "I run a SaaS company without employees. Here's how, and here are the real numbers."
- Prepare 3 core stories: (1) how Claude Code wrote the product, (2) how AI handles growth and marketing, (3) why NIS2 is the perfect first market for this experiment

#### Events
- **NIS2 compliance events** in Spain (priority) — sponsor a speaking slot, be the "practical tool" voice among compliance consultants
- **IT events and trade shows**: CyberSec Spain, IT Manager Forum, ISACA Spain chapter events
- **Startup events**: South Summit, 4YFN (Barcelona), Slush Madrid equivalent
- **AI/future-of-work events**: ideal stage for the "AI-managed company" story
- Format: talk + live demo. Title: "Cómo gestionar una empresa de software sin empleados"

#### Courses & Educational Content
- **Short course (free):** "How to prepare your IT asset inventory for NIS2" — builds credibility and generates leads
- Platform: LinkedIn Learning, YouTube, or a simple Gumroad landing page
- **Email mini-course (5 days):** "5 steps to NIS2 IT asset compliance" — captures emails, filters serious buyers
- **Long-term:** a paid course on "AI-orchestrated company management" — for startup founders, not just IT managers

## Distribution Layers

### Layer 1: Open Source Product
- The self-hosted version is 100% free and open source (AGPL)
- IT professionals and sysadmins can install it with zero cost
- Self-hosters become word-of-mouth advocates and eventual cloud customers
- GitHub stars, forks, and issues are leading indicators of awareness

### Layer 2: The AI-Managed Company Story
- The `startup/` folder in the repo documents everything: team, business model, financials, pricing decisions, GTM strategy
- This folder is as much a marketing asset as the product
- Target audiences: developers building SaaS, IT professionals evaluating tools, founders exploring AI-augmented models, journalists covering AI/future of work
- The story runs in parallel with the product — both are worth following
- Regular content:
  - "Week X: what I asked Claude to build and what it shipped"
  - "Month X MRR: here are the numbers, here is what worked and what didn't"
  - "Why I have advisors instead of employees — and how that works"

### Layer 3: Podcasts + Events + Courses (First 6 Months Focus)
- See "First 6 Months" section above
- The "AI-managed company" angle is compelling enough to get podcast slots without a PR agency
- Events position us as the practical NIS2 compliance tool, not just another helpdesk
- Courses generate leads and establish domain authority

### Layer 4: SEO & Content
- Target keywords: "open source ITSM", "NIS2 IT asset management", "helpdesk software SMB Spain", "ITAM software NIS2 compliance"
- Content: comparison articles, setup guides, NIS2 use case posts
- Long-term compounding asset — articles written today rank for years

### Layer 5: Community Presence
- r/sysadmin, r/selfhosted, r/homelab
- LinkedIn — IT managers, ops leads, CTOs at Spanish SMBs
- Hacker News — developers and technical founders
- Product Hunt — launch moment
- GitHub Trending — quality repo visibility

### Layer 6: Product-Led Growth (PLG)
- Free plan and open source plan are permanent — not trials
- Users upgrade when they hit company size limits or need cloud-hosted convenience
- The free plan's job is to create advocates, not just leads

## Advisor Network (Instead of Hiring)

When human expertise is needed, we bring in advisors:
- **NIS2 / cybersecurity compliance experts** — validate product features and compliance claims
- **IT sector sales advisors** — help close first enterprise deals
- **Community connectors** — people with access to IT manager networks in Spain

Advisors are compensated with equity or small retainers. No operational overhead, no full-time salaries. Human perspective on demand.

## What We Will Not Do

- Pay for ads before organic channels are saturated
- Buy GitHub stars or fake reviews
- Pretend to be bigger than we are — the build in public narrative only works if it's honest
- Chase press coverage as a vanity metric — we chase signups and upgrades
- Hire employees to replace AI agents until product-market fit and revenue are proven
