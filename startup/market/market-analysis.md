**Date:** 2026-02-22

## The Real Competitor is Excel

**67% of EU SMBs still manage IT assets in spreadsheets or manually.** Only 12% have a mature IT management framework. The primary competitor is not Freshservice or Lansweeper — it is a shared Google Sheet with a list of laptops.

This means the sales conversation is not "why switch from competitor X" — it is "why stop using a spreadsheet." NIS2 answers that question better than any feature comparison.

### Why Spain First

- Home market — no translation needed, no cultural barrier
- CEO's professional network is in Spain
- Spanish SMBs are heavily represented in NIS2 Annex sectors: food & beverage, manufacturing, logistics, health
- Relatively low ITAM software penetration vs. Germany or Netherlands
- 100% online sales — no geographic constraint within Spain

### Spain Go-To-Market

### DORA (Financial Sector)

- ~22,000 financial entities in the EU in full scope
- ~8,000–12,000 SMBs (fintechs, regional banks, insurance brokers, investment firms)
- DORA requires ICT asset tracking and incident reporting — direct product fit
- Potential to create a "DORA compliance" specific landing page and feature set

### CRA (Cyber Resilience Act — from 2027)

- ~50,000–150,000 EU SMB manufacturers in scope
- Requires Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) and product lifecycle tracking
- Reporting obligations start September 2026 — within our Year 1
- Future roadmap opportunity: SBOM module, CRA-specific reports

## The Compliance Wave Timeline

```
Oct 2024  — NIS2 enforceable. Companies start receiving letters.
Jan 2025  — DORA active. Financial sector scrambling.
Mar 2026  — DeskSupportMonkey launches.
Sep 2026  — First audit cycles. Companies realize they need documentation NOW.
Sep 2027  — CRA reporting obligations begin.
Dec 2027  — Full compliance boom. NIS2 + DORA + CRA all active simultaneously.
```

The product launches 18 months before peak demand. That is the right time — early enough to build community and trust, late enough that the regulation is real and companies feel the pressure.
