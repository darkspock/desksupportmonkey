## The Problem

Every company with more than 10 employees has the same problem: nobody knows exactly what devices they have, who has them, or what happened to them last year.

IT managers track assets in spreadsheets. Incidents get reported in WhatsApp. Purchases live in someone's email inbox. When an auditor shows up and asks "show me your IT asset inventory and the incidents associated with each device" — there is nothing to show.

This was manageable when compliance was optional. It is not manageable anymore.

**NIS2 is now enforceable across the EU.** It explicitly requires organizations to maintain a documented IT asset inventory, link incidents to specific assets, and demonstrate control over every device that touches their network. DORA adds the same requirements for financial services. The CRA is coming for hardware manufacturers.

Hundreds of thousands of European companies have a legal obligation they cannot currently meet.

---

## The Solution

DeskSupportMonkey manages the complete lifecycle of every device in your company:

**Purchase → Warehouse → Assignment → Incidents → Decommission**

Every device has a full history: who ordered it, when it arrived, who it was assigned to, every incident it has ever had, and when it was retired. That history is your NIS2 audit trail.

It is not a generic helpdesk. Tickets exist only for assets — every incident is tied to a specific device, not a generic support queue.

---

## Why Now

- **NIS2** became enforceable in October 2024
- **DORA** took effect January 2025 (financial sector)
- **CRA** is phasing in through 2027
- Most affected companies have nothing in place
- The auditors are starting to ask questions

The demand is created by law, not by marketing.
