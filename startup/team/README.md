# Team

**Headcount:** 5
**Philosophy:** Generalists first. Every hire covers at least two domains. Hiring is driven by revenue, not funding rounds.

---

## Org Chart

```
CEO (Co-founder)
 ├── CTO (Co-founder)
 │    └── Senior Full-Stack Engineer
 ├── Head of Growth
 └── Head of Customer Success & Operations
```

## Hiring Sequence

1. CEO + CTO — Day 0 (co-founders)
2. Senior Full-Stack Engineer — Pre-launch, when CTO needs dev capacity
3. Head of Growth — At launch or first paying customers
4. Head of Customer Success & Ops — When customer base grows past 10 companies
