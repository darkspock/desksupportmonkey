# Head of Customer Success & Operations

## Role Summary

This person keeps customers happy, renewing, and expanding. They own the post-sale journey — from onboarding to QBRs to renewal. They also run internal operations: billing admin, vendor management, and the day-to-day processes that don't belong to Engineering or Growth. In a small startup, they are the glue.

---

## Responsibilities

### Customer Onboarding
- Run onboarding calls for new companies (admin setup, data import, team invite)
- Build and maintain onboarding materials (docs, videos, templates)
- Ensure new customers hit their first value milestone within 7 days

### Customer Success
- Own a portfolio of active customers
- Conduct monthly check-ins and quarterly business reviews (QBRs) for Premium/Enterprise accounts
- Identify expansion opportunities (upsell, additional seats)
- Proactively flag churn risk to CEO and Growth

### Support
- First line of support for all inbound tickets (email, in-app chat)
- Triage bugs vs. feature requests — route to CTO with context
- Maintain the help center / knowledge base
- Define and track SLA response times

### Operations
- Manage vendor relationships (Stripe, Sentry, hosting providers, etc.)
- Maintain internal wiki and process documentation
- Handle billing edge cases and manual plan adjustments (via super-admin tools)
- Assist CEO with investor reporting and data room updates

### Voice of the Customer
- Collect and synthesize product feedback from customers
- Run NPS surveys and report results to the team
- Represent the customer in product prioritization discussions

---

## Skills Required

- Customer-facing experience in B2B SaaS (CS, account management, or support)
- Strong written communication — can handle complex issues over email with clarity
- Organized and process-oriented — builds playbooks, not just handles fires
- Basic data skills — can pull a churn report from a spreadsheet or CRM
- Comfortable with technical products — doesn't need to code but understands APIs, integrations, user roles
- Bonus: experience with ITSM, HR tech, or operations software

---

## Metrics Owned

- NPS / CSAT
- Churn rate (logo and revenue)
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
- Time to first value (onboarding milestone)
- Support ticket resolution time (SLA)

---

## Ideal Background

Customer Success Manager or Account Manager at a B2B SaaS company serving SMBs. Has run onboarding programs, written help documentation, and managed renewal conversations. Comfortable being the second person a customer talks to after sales.
