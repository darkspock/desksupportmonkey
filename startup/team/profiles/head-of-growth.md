# Head of Growth

## Role Summary

This person grows the business without burning cash. Their core skill is finding and exploiting channels that cost time, not money — SEO, community, content, cold outreach, product virality, partnerships. In a bootstrapped company, paid ads are the last resort, not the default. Every euro of CAC has to earn its place.

They are creative, scrappy, and obsessed with compounding effects. They prefer a strategy that takes 3 months to kick in but costs nothing over a campaign that produces results this week and nothing next month.

---

## Philosophy

- **The product is open source — the startup is too** — the entire codebase, architecture, business model, team profiles, financials, and go-to-market strategy are public on GitHub. Building in public is the distribution strategy.
- **Distribution is a product** — the best marketing is a product so useful that IT professionals share it with each other
- **Organic before paid** — build channels that grow without a budget before spending a single euro on ads
- **Content is infrastructure** — a well-ranked article brings leads forever; a paid ad stops the moment you stop paying
- **Community over campaigns** — being present and useful in IT forums, subreddits, LinkedIn groups, and Slack communities where the ICP hangs out beats any campaign
- **Measure everything, spend nothing you can't justify** — CAC must be known for every channel; channels that don't pay back get cut

---

## Responsibilities

### Organic & Content Marketing
- Own SEO strategy — keyword research targeting IT managers, sysadmins, helpdesk leads
- Write or produce content that ranks: comparison articles, "best ITSM tools for SMB", how-to guides, templates
- Maintain the company blog and keep it technically credible (the audience is IT professionals — fluff gets ignored)
- Build a backlink strategy through genuine contributions: guest posts, tool directories, integrations pages

### Community & Social Presence
- Be present where the ICP lives: r/sysadmin, IT forums, LinkedIn groups, Slack communities, Discord servers
- Contribute genuinely — answer questions, share useful content, never spam
- Build the CEO's LinkedIn as a thought leadership channel (ghostwrite if needed)
- Identify and nurture brand advocates among early customers

### Product-Led Growth (PLG)
- Optimize the free plan → paid upgrade funnel with the CTO
- Design in-app upgrade triggers based on usage signals (hitting limits, using premium-adjacent features)
- Run activation experiments to reduce time-to-value for new signups
- Build a referral or word-of-mouth mechanism into the product

### Outbound (Zero-Cost)
- Run cold outreach sequences targeting IT managers and ops leads at SMBs — email and LinkedIn
- Write sequences that are relevant and specific, not generic — personalization at scale using AI
- Own the CRM and pipeline; qualify leads before handing to CEO for demos

### Build in Public
- Own the "build in public" narrative on GitHub, LinkedIn, and tech communities
- The `startup/` folder in the repo is public — share it. It contains team profiles, business model, financials, GTM strategy, everything. This is the content.
- Document the journey: architectural decisions, mistakes, MRR milestones, hiring decisions — all public
- Turn the open source community into a distribution engine: contributors become advocates, advocates become customers or refer customers
- Submit the project to Hacker News (Show HN), Product Hunt, and open source directories
- Engage with every GitHub star, issue, and fork — these are warm leads

### Partnerships & Distribution
- Identify and close distribution partnerships: MSPs, IT consultancies, software directories (Capterra, G2, Product Hunt, AlternativeTo)
- Get listed on every relevant free directory and review platform
- Build integrations or partnerships that create mutual referral flows

### Analytics
- Track CAC by channel — organic, outbound, community, referral, partner
- Report weekly on free signups, free → paid conversion, and MRR from growth channels
- Cut what doesn't work fast; double down on what does

---

## Skills Required

- SEO — deep: technical SEO, on-page, link building, keyword strategy
- Content writing — can write for a technical audience without being boring
- Cold outreach — high open rates, not spray-and-pray
- Community building — genuine participation, not just promotion
- PLG mechanics — knows how to design freemium funnels and in-app upsell flows
- AI-assisted marketing — uses AI to produce content, personalize outreach, and analyze data at 10x the speed
- Analytics — knows which numbers matter and which are vanity
- Resourceful with tools: can get 80% of the value of a €500/month tool from a free alternative

---

## What This Person Does Not Do

- Does not run paid ads as a default strategy — only when organic channels are saturated and the unit economics are proven
- Does not buy followers, links, or engagement
- Does not produce content for content's sake — every piece must target a specific keyword or audience
- Does not spend without a clear hypothesis and measurement plan

---

## Metrics Owned

- Organic traffic growth (MoM)
- Free signups from organic channels
- Free → paid conversion rate
- CAC by channel (target: as close to €0 as possible on organic)
- MRR attributed to Growth
- LinkedIn / community engagement reach
